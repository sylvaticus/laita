Grammar and style checking, and on-demand rewriting, from a large language model you run
yourself with Ollama.

Writer gets a proofreader that underlines problems as you type and offers a fix on
click - only in the paragraph you are editing, unless you ask it to check the whole
document. Writer, Calc, Impress and Draw all get "Transform selection": describe what you
want done to the selected text ("make this formal", "translate to French", "shorten"), and
the result is written back or appended.

Nothing is sent anywhere except to the Ollama endpoint you configure. That endpoint is
http://localhost:11434 by default, so with the default settings and a model you have
pulled locally, your text does not leave your machine. Point it at a remote Ollama, or
choose one of Ollama's cloud models, and it does - the choice is yours and it is visible
in the settings.

You need Ollama installed and a model pulled - the default is qwen3.5:9b, so: ollama pull qwen3.5:9b.

After installing, enable it for your language:
Tools - Options - Languages and Locales - Writing Aids - Available Language Modules -
Edit... - choose your language - tick LAITA.

Settings are under Tools - Options - LAITA - Local AI Text Assistant. The commands
are in the LAITA menu, and on the LAITA toolbar if you turn it on under View -
Toolbars.
